# Data Analysis Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report provides an analysis of a dataset comprising 891 samples with 12 features each, focusing on data quality, key findings, and recommendations. Significant quality issues were identified in the dataset, including high percentages of missing values in the 'Age' and 'Cabin' columns. Exploratory data analysis (EDA) has supported claims of correlation between fare and survival rates, variations in survival based on passenger class, sex, and familial connections. No visualizations were requested, but the findings provide a comprehensive understanding of survival determinants for passengers.

## 2. Dataset Overview
The dataset consists of 891 entries with the following relevant columns:
- **PassengerId**: Unique identifier for passengers.
- **Name**: Passenger names.
- **Age**: Passenger age, with notable missing entries.
- **Cabin**: Cabin number, with the highest missing values.
- **Embarked**: Point of embarkation with minimal missing values.
- **Ticket**: Ticket information with inconsistent formatting.
- **Pclass, Sex, SibSp**: Categorical data with low cardinality, offering insights into passenger classes, gender distribution, and relationships onboard.

## 3. Data Quality
Significant issues identified:
- **High Missing Values**:
  - 'Age' has 177 missing values (~19.87%).
  - 'Cabin' shows 77.1% missing values deemed crucial for analysis.
  - 'Embarked' shows a negligible number of missing values (0.22%).
- **Uniqueness**: 'PassengerId' and 'Name' exhibit expected uniqueness, posing no immediate concern.
- **Inconsistent Formatting**: 'Ticket' column presents variable formatting with 681 unique entries, hindering comprehensive analysis.

## 4. Key Findings
1. **Correlation between Fare and Survival**: 
   - Supported by a correlation coefficient of 0.2573, indicating a low to moderate positive correlation.
  
2. **Differences in Survival Rates by Class**:
   - First class: 62.96% survival rate.
   - Second class: 47.28% survival rate.
   - Third class: 24.24% survival rate, highlighting class-based survival disparities with high confidence.
  
3. **Gender Differences in Survival**:
   - Female survival rate is significantly higher at 74.2% compared to males at 18.89%, supported by strong statistical evidence.

4. **Impact of Siblings/Spouses**:
   - Survival rates show variations based on the number of siblings/spouses aboard, with notable higher survival rates for passengers with 1 sibling/spouse at 53.59%.
